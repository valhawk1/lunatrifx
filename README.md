### Felicitations curious and esteemed patrons of the hardware that is soft! I am perpetually striving to deconstruct and pursue new knowledge of the vast unknown.

I'm a recent graduate of THE San Diego State University.

## 🔭 Current

I'm part of the second ever SWAN Institute cohort, created by UC San Diego. The cohort emphasizes refreshers and new knowledge on hardware engineering, particularly semiconductors and fabrication. Outside of this, GitHub and software is a hobby where I learn to reverse engineer projects I come across and contribute to open-source projects (to claim a sense of ownership).

## ⚡️ Experience

I am deeply inspired to create projects which allows me to have deep ownership, think different and frequently grow, and ultimately have something that lasts for a long time. If that sounds familiar, my inspiration starts with NASA's Voyager probes. Something that should have lasted only 5 years is going on strong for 50 years and counting. Engineers have found countless ways, and sometimes invented new ways, to build/save/reconstruct something that's out there. That's my mantra!

I am the creator of Addlyn, a CV model I've created when I was 15. It's a hardware engineering project starting from my third year of high school, which saw the earliest versions of voice captures and transcription, pre-AI/LLM.
